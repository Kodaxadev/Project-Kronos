# AI Use Disclosure

Project Kronos is intentionally AI-assisted. The maintainer is a nonexpert in the underlying mathematics and code.

AI systems may propose mathematics, write software, produce audits, formalize statements, and prepare documentation. Their output is treated as untrusted until supported by the evidence type claimed. AI provenance does not establish independent reproduction, external review, novelty, or correctness.

The experimental question is whether accumulated orchestration practices and Cruthúnas-style controls can transfer into a new mathematical domain while preserving honest evidence boundaries.
