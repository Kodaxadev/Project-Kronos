## Intake cautions

- The 2024 prime-length paper states that even lengths at weight three are settled while odd lengths remain incomplete, and that prime lengths are fundamental.
- It reports that Conjecture B had been computationally checked for primes through `2^30` in earlier work and proves a class where the quotient index is an odd prime.
- These are literature reports until the underlying papers, statements, and computations are independently reconciled.
